# 6. Company Philosophy

RoboDev Labs is built around a few fundamental beliefs.

Important company knowledge should be documented.

Documentation should reduce dependency on individual people and make the organization easier to operate as it grows.

### Systems Before Scale

### Long-Term Thinking

Decisions should be evaluated against the goal of building a professional company over many years.

## Logo concept

The RoboDev Labs identity uses an **R/S negative-space concept**.

The design is based around the letter **R** as the primary visible form, while the negative space of the R creates an **S**.

Conceptually:

**R remains visible.**  
**S exists within the negative space.**

This creates a single mark containing two letterforms.

The design should remain simple enough to work at very small sizes while retaining its meaning at larger sizes.

## Avoid

Do not use:

- Robot heads
- Gears
- Coding brackets
- Light bulbs
